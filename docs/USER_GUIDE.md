# Customer guide

This guide explains how a customer uses the Sawyun Ecommerce shop. Staff can use it to answer
customer questions. Screens look slightly different on a phone (bottom tab bar: Home, Categories,
Cart, Account) and on a computer (links in the top header), but the steps are the same.

## 1. Browsing

- **Home page** — at the top you see running deals and events (with a countdown to when they end), then categories, *Popular right now*, *Featured picks* and *New arrivals*.
- **Products** — shows all products. Use the search box in the header to search by name. Use **Filters** to choose a category, a price range in baht, or *In stock only*, and **Sort by** most popular, newest, price (low → high or high → low) or top rated. *Clear filters* resets everything.
- **Categories** and **Deals & events** — browse by category, or open an event to see all its products.
- **Product page** — swipe or click through the photos, choose an **option** (for example colour and size), set the quantity and press **Add to cart** or **Buy now**. A red badge such as *-25%* means the price is lower than the original (struck-through) price. *Only 3 left* means stock is low. Reviews from other customers are at the bottom.

Prices on product pages do **not** include VAT. VAT (7%) and delivery are added at checkout.

## 2. Changing the language

Use the language switch in the header (**English / မြန်မာ**). The whole site changes immediately,
including product names and descriptions where a Myanmar translation exists (otherwise the English
text is shown). The browser remembers your choice for the next visit.

## 3. The cart

- You can add products to the cart **without signing in**. The cart is saved in this browser only (it does not move to your phone or another computer).
- Change the quantity with **−** / **+** (up to 99 per item, and not more than the available stock), or remove an item.
- Each time you open the cart, prices and stock are checked again. If something changed you will see a message on the item, for example *Only 2 available — please lower the quantity* (press **Set to 2**) or *Out of stock — please remove it*. You cannot check out until these are fixed.
- The cart shows the subtotal only; delivery and VAT are calculated at checkout.

## 4. Signing in (no password)

1. Press **Sign in** (or **Sign in to check out** in the cart).
2. Type your email and press **Send code**.
3. Open your email. The code is a **6-digit number**, valid for **5 minutes**. If you can't find it, check the spam folder.
4. Type the code and press **Verify & sign in**.

If you have never signed in before, an account is created automatically.

Good to know:

- You can ask for a new code after **60 seconds** (the button shows a countdown). You can request at most 5 codes per hour.
- After **5 wrong codes**, that code stops working — request a new one.
- You stay signed in on this device for up to 30 days, or until you have not visited for 7 days.

## 5. Checkout

1. From the cart press **Checkout**.
2. **Delivery address** — pick a saved address, or choose *Deliver to a new address*. For a new address, choose the **Province**, then **District** and **Subdistrict** from the lists; the **Postcode** fills in automatically. Tick *Save this address to my account* to reuse it next time.
3. **Coupon code** — type it and press **Apply**. If the code cannot be used you will see why (for example *Your order doesn't meet this coupon's minimum spend* or *This coupon has expired*).
4. **Payment method** — *PromptPay QR* or *Cash on delivery*. Cash on delivery is only offered when the total is at or below the shop's limit (default ฿10,000).
5. Optional **Note for the shop** (delivery instructions, gift message — up to 500 characters).
6. Check the **Order summary**: subtotal, discount, delivery (with the zone name, or *Free*), VAT 7% and **Total**. If you are close to free delivery the page tells you how much more to add.
7. Press **Place order**.

The items are now reserved for you. If something sold out a moment ago, you will see a message and
the summary updates — adjust the cart and try again.

## 6. Paying with PromptPay and uploading the slip

After placing a PromptPay order you go straight to the **Pay with PromptPay** page.

1. Open any Thai banking app and **scan the QR code** (or press *Save QR image* and scan it from your photo gallery in the banking app).
2. The amount is already filled in. Check that the **amount** and **account name** match the page, then confirm the transfer.
3. Take a screenshot of the transfer slip (or use the slip image the bank app saves).
4. Back on the pay page, choose the slip image (**JPG, PNG or WebP, up to 5 MB**) and press **Send slip**.

The order status becomes **Verifying payment**. Shop staff check the slip against their bank account,
usually within a few minutes during business hours, and the status becomes **Paid**.

Important:

- Pay before the **time left** countdown ends (default 30 minutes). If no slip arrives in time the order becomes **Expired** and the items are released. Place a new order if you still want them.
- If staff **reject** the slip (for example the image is unreadable or the amount is wrong), the pay page shows the reason and gives you a **new** time window to upload a correct slip.
- You can upload a slip only while the order is *Awaiting payment*. While staff are verifying, you cannot upload another one.
- If you leave the page, open **Account → Orders**, choose the order and press **Pay now**.

## 7. Cash on delivery

Choose *Cash on delivery* at checkout. The order is **Confirmed** straight away. Pay the courier in
cash when the parcel arrives.

## 8. Tracking your orders

Open **Account → Orders**. Filter by status if you have many orders. Each order shows:

- the **progress timeline** with every status change and its time;
- items, delivery address, payment method and the price breakdown;
- payment slips you uploaded and their status (with the staff note if rejected);
- **Shipment**: once shipped, the courier name, tracking number and a **Track parcel** link (if the shop added one).

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Awaiting payment | PromptPay order waiting for your payment and slip. |
| Verifying payment | Your slip is being checked. |
| Paid | Payment approved. |
| Confirmed | Cash-on-delivery order accepted. |
| Packing | The shop is packing your parcel. |
| Shipped | Handed to the courier — use the tracking number. |
| Delivered | Arrived. You can now review the products. |
| Cancelled | Cancelled by you or the shop. |
| Expired | Payment was not received in time. |

**Cancelling:** while the order is *Awaiting payment*, *Verifying payment* or *Confirmed* (COD), press
**Cancel order** and optionally give a reason. After that, only the shop can cancel. If you already
paid and need a refund, contact the shop (support email/phone in the footer); refunds are handled
outside the website.

## 9. Reviews

After an order is **Delivered**, open it and press **Write a review** next to a product (or open the
product page and press **Write a review**). Choose 1–5 stars and optionally write a comment (up to
1,000 characters). You can review each product once. Your review appears on the product page after
the shop approves it.

## 10. Wishlist

Press the **heart** on a product card or product page to save it (you need to be signed in). See all
saved products in **Account → Wishlist**. Press the heart again to remove it. Products that the shop
stops selling disappear from the wishlist.

## 11. Profile and addresses

- **Account → Profile** — change your name and phone number. Your sign-in email cannot be changed.
- **Account → Addresses** — add, edit or delete addresses (up to 20). One address is the **default** and is pre-selected at checkout; use **Set as default** to change it. Your first address automatically becomes the default.

## 12. Security page

**Account → Security** lists every device signed in to your account (browser, system, IP address,
when it signed in and when it was last active). *This device* marks the one you are using.

- Press **Sign out** next to a device you don't recognise.
- Press **Sign out all other devices** if you think someone else used your email.
- If you lose access to your email, contact the shop; an admin can disable the account.
